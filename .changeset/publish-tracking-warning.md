Publish answers with a `warnings` list, and the editor shows each warning after the success toast. A Publish whose version tracks an Entity while runs an untracked version started are still in flight, or the reverse, now warns with the number of those runs: they identify their Entity in the other form, so they finish on their old version and Migration refuses them. The warning never stops the Publish.
